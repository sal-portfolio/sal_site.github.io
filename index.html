S. Amanda Levis

Data Analytics  ·  Decision Science  ·  Behavioral Economics

After receiving my PhD from Yale in 2018, I used my knowledge of behavioral economics to establish the first applied behavioral science team at Vanguard. There, my work combined data analytics with client psychology to answer questions in the domain of financial literacy and wellness. I conducted large field experiments, designed analytic tools, and led cross-functional teams to inform digital product strategy. I am currently open to consulting or full-time opportunities, remote preferable.
