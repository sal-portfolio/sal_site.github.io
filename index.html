Sarah Amanda Levis

Data Analytics  ·  Decision Science  ·  Behavioral Economics

After receiving my PhD from Yale in 2018, I used my knowledge of behavioral economics to establish the first applied behavioral science team at Vanguard. There, my work combined data analytics with client psychology to answer big questions in the domain of financial literacy and wellness. I conducted large field experiments, designed analytic tools, and led cross-functional teams to inform digital product strategy. In late 2023, I decided to take a risk and explore consulting work. My industry consulting blends a deep understanding of human decision-making processes with a broad statistical toolkit.
